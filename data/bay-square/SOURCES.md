- Exact shop unit numbers remain UNKNOWN unless indicated by a primary source. Search/mapping may be based on venue address rather than a specific door.
- Company sites may refer to marketing or sales offices rather than registered headquarters.
- No claim of full coverage, external consent, trademark licenses, legal status or operator endorsement.
