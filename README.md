# Dubai Business Atlas
**Independent multilingual research on Dubai businesses — English · Русский · العربية**

**Repository status:** PUBLIC DATA PILOT / NO LIVE WEBSITE / NO DEPLOYMENT PIPELINE

Dubai Business Atlas is an independent project cataloguing publicly described companies, competencies, offices, and services across Dubai. **Bay Square in Business Bay is the first pilot district.** We are not Bay Square management and are not endorsed by the venue or by listed businesses.

### English
The initial set includes **48 provisional Bay Square company/business records** with linked sources and evidence tiers. These are research candidates, not a confirmed current tenant register. Addresses, services and business details may be outdated and should be reconfirmed with primary sources. The website is **not yet deployed**.

## Repository structure
- `data/bay-square/companies.json` — a filtered public copy of source-linked provisional company data.
- `data/bay-square/SOURCES.md` — source and verification caveats.
- `prototype/bay-square/` — **existing** archived static prototype migrated from previous work. It is not newly developed in this migration and is not deployed.
- `docs/PUBLICATION_STATUS.md` — current public state and proposed publication quality gate.

Research and uncertain working notes are handled in a **separate private repository**, not here. New public exports must pass provenance, privacy, translation, location and licensing checks. No automated daily publishing is configured yet.

**Origin:** Public-safe extract of an internal Bay Square pilot as of 2026-10-12. Detailed source-commit provenance and the full research archive are retained only in the private research workspace; the original repository history was not imported.

**Privacy:** No owner's private contacts, meeting records, business cards, client information or private AI OS instructions belong in this repository.
