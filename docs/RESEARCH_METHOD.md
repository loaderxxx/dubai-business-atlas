# Public research method

Research pass: 2026-11-12.

1. Preserve all 48 stable record IDs and historical location evidence.
2. Read venue operator detail pages and official company pages; search is discovery rather than proof.
3. Use source-specific support notes. Indexed official content is qualified where live retrieval fails.
4. Add short original descriptions, not scraped marketing copy. Customer segmentation is editorial inference.
5. Do not infer closure from an inaccessible website or omission from a directory.
6. Separate company-wide services from branch availability; do not promise prices, stock or licenses.
7. Prefer unknown fields over guessed office units or inferred legal-entity matches.
8. Export only public business facts, without personal contacts, photos, credentials or private work.
9. Keep the archive prototype and deployment unchanged.
10. Validate IDs, counts, links, required provenance fields and absence of sensitive-contact exports.

Limitations: this is a bounded desk-research pass, not an exhaustive census of office tenants. Sources can be stale even when retrieved today. Primary-source claims are not independent certifications. Full EN/RU/AR copy editing, business-license checks and on-site occupancy checks remain future work.
